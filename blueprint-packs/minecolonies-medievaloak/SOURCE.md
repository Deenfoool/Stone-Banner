# MineColonies Medieval Oak starter houses

Two **unmodified** Structurize v1 blueprint files: `residence1.blueprint` and
`residence2.blueprint` from MineColonies' Medieval Oak style by **Carlansor**.

Upstream: https://github.com/ldtteam/minecolonies
Commit: `4eb1b870b7cb2339b79f231033df5bbef27b2646`
Original directory: `src/main/resources/blueprints/minecolonies/medievaloak/fundamentals/`
Style attribution: `src/main/resources/blueprints/minecolonies/medievaloak/pack.json`

This optional data pack is distributed separately from the Stone & Banner mod JAR.
Its upstream license is **GNU GPL v3**; the full license is included as `LICENSE`.
The `.blueprint` files themselves are the original editable source data. No upstream
Java code or asset images are included. The independent reader and runtime vanilla
adapters live in Stone & Banner; the original files remain unchanged.

Extract this pack into the Minecraft instance/server folder, merging `config/`.
Restart the server/world. In Stone & Banner press **B**, then use the catalog arrows.
The two houses use the reader's explicit vanilla adaptation for supported decorative
blocks. Hut markers are empty space, inventories and entities are not imported.
The catalog reports unavailable blocks; large/tall builds may require player-built
stairs or working platforms. Installation does not require MineColonies or DO.
