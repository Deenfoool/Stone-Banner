# Stone & Banner HUD assets

The pixel art in `terrain-atlas.svg` and vector symbols in `hud-icons.svg` were authored specifically for the standalone Stone & Banner HUD playground. They are not Mojang/Minecraft textures, and the sandbox does not bundle Minecraft jar assets. All resources are self-hosted by the repository, with no external CDN or third-party font requirements.

The sprite dimensions and tile positions are in `atlas.json` (32×32 pixels each, 6 columns). These assets are separate from the Java/Forge mod and do not change its renderer.
